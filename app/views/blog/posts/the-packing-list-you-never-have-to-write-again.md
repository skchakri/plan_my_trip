Somewhere on your phone is a notes app with four packing lists in it. They're all almost identical. Each one was written the night before a trip, from scratch, by a version of you who was sure they'd remember everything this time. Each trip, something still got left on the kitchen counter.

Packing lists are the most re-invented document in travel. So when Wanderply builds a trip, it builds the list too.

## Built from the trip, not from a template

A generic packing template gives you "toiletries" and calls it a day. Wanderply's checklist is generated *from your actual plan* — the same build that schedules your days also looks at what those days contain.

A real hike on day two puts trail shoes and extra water on the list. A desert weekend adds sun protection and layers for the overnight temperature swing, because the per-day weather is part of the plan. Traveling with kids? The back-seat survival kit shows up without you asking. The list knows what trip it belongs to.

![A hiker sitting on a rock, writing notes](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1519160558534-579f5106e43f?w=1200&q=80)

## Sectioned, assigned, and satisfying to tap

The checklist lives at its own page inside every trip — sectioned by category, with a progress bar that fills as you pack. Each item toggles with a single tap, and items can carry a *person*, so "who was supposed to pack the tent" stops being a rest-stop argument and starts being a matter of record.

Need something the generator didn't think of? There's an inline add form right on the page. Grandma's birthday present goes on the list next to the sunscreen, where it belongs.

## It works in the garage, and at the trailhead

Here's the detail that makes it a tool instead of a toy: once you've opened your trip online, the plan and checklist **keep working offline**. The last-loaded copy is cached on your phone, so you can stand in the garage at 6am — or at a trailhead with zero bars — ticking things off with the trunk open.

That's also why it's a checklist and not a printout. The list travels with the trip, updates when the trip does, and is the same one every member of the trip sees.
