A lot of travel apps make their money by funneling you into *their* booking. Wanderply doesn't. We're not a booking site and never wanted to be. What we can do is make the money side of a trip less annoying — surface the discounts you're actually entitled to, keep the group's spending fair, and warn you when a flight moves.

## Booking links that know your memberships

On every trip, Wanderply generates hotel, car, flight, and activity **search links** — and where you qualify, it badges the **member rate** right on them. Not scraped promo codes that expired six months ago; only the real, recurring programs:

- **Hilton Honors**, **Marriott** member rates
- **Booking.com Genius**
- **Costco Travel**, **AAA**, **AARP**
- **Best Price Guarantee**, **AutoSlash** for car rentals

Tell Wanderply which programs you're in (once, in your account) and it only shows the badges that apply to you. No noise, no "you might save" — just the rate you can actually get.

### Stack your points, too

If you carry a travel card, Wanderply surfaces the **Chase, Amex, or Capital One travel portals** as a separate "stack points" panel — so you can decide when booking through a portal is worth it versus booking direct. It's a nudge, not a sales pitch.

## Split the trip fairly

Trips cost money, and someone always fronts the campsite fee while someone else covers gas. Wanderply's **expense tracking** lets everyone on the trip log what they spent, and it works out **who owes whom** at the end. No separate app, no shared spreadsheet that breaks the moment someone edits the wrong cell — it lives right on the trip everyone's already looking at.

## Flight alerts, opt-in

If your plan includes a flight, Wanderply can watch it for you. Turn on **flight-status alerts** and you'll know when the gate changes or the departure slips — before you're standing at the wrong end of the terminal. It's off by default and opt-in per reservation, because nobody needs more notifications they didn't ask for.

## The philosophy: your trip, your money

Everything here follows one rule — Wanderply works for *you*, not for a hotel chain's affiliate desk. We show you the discount, split the bill, and watch the flight. We don't decide where you sleep, and we don't take a cut of pretending to.
