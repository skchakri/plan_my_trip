11:00
AM
Stop 1 · Eagle Point · 90 min
The Skywalk
Shuttle to Eagle Point. Stow phones/bags in free lockers, put on foot booties (provided), step onto the glass. 4,000 ft straight down to the Colorado River. The bridge holds 70 fully-loaded 747s — completely safe, just unforgettable.
Photo strategy One person stays off the bridge with a phone/camera and shoots the rest of the family on it. Then swap. That way you get free photos and skip the $69 print package.
Eagle Point bonus The cliff itself looks like an eagle (you can see it from the Sky View Restaurant). At 11 AM Fri/Sat/Sun there are Hualapai Bird Singers performances — included with admission.

Shuttle ride from Eagle Point. Different angle of the canyon, panoramic views, old guano mining tram remnants. Light hiking — short trail, manageable for parents but skip if anyone is tired.
